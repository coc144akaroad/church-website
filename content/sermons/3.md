<h3>Scripture Reference: 2 Corinthians 12:9</h3>
<p><strong>"My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness."</strong></p>
<p>We live in a world that glorifies strength and self-sufficiency. Yet the Christian message is countercultural: God's grace is most powerful in our weakness. This truth revolutionized Paul's understanding of his faith, and it can transform ours as well.</p>
<h3>What Is God's Grace?</h3>
<p>Grace is God's unmerited favor—the blessing we receive not because we've earned it, but because God loves us. It's what saves us through Christ, and it's what sustains us daily. Grace isn't just for our conversion; it's the continuous supply of God's strength, provision, and love throughout our lives.</p>
<h3>The Paradox of Weakness and Strength</h3>
<p>When Paul asked God to remove his thorn in the flesh (a source of suffering and weakness), God didn't remove it. Instead, God promised that His grace would be sufficient. In his weakness, Paul learned to depend on God's strength. This principle applies to us: our limitations, struggles, and failures become opportunities for God to demonstrate His power.</p>
<h3>Living by Grace Daily</h3>
<p>Grace sustains us through illness, loss, failure, and uncertainty. When you feel inadequate, remember that God's strength is perfected in your weakness. When you stumble, grace picks you up. When you're exhausted, grace provides renewal. This is not a one-time gift but a continuous stream of God's sustaining power.</p>
<p>Whatever you're facing today, God's grace is sufficient. Lean into it, trust it, and experience the victory that comes through resting in God's power.</p>
