<h3>Scripture Reference: 2 Corinthians 5:7</h3>
<p><strong>"For we live by faith, not by sight."</strong></p>
<p>In our Christian journey, we often encounter situations that test our faith. The world tells us to trust only what we can see, measure, and control. However, the apostle Paul reminds us that living by faith means relying on God's promises even when circumstances appear challenging.</p>
<h3>Understanding Faith</h3>
<p>Faith is not blind hope or wishful thinking. Rather, it is a confident trust in God's character and His Word. Throughout Scripture, we see examples of men and women who walked by faith—Abraham leaving his homeland, Moses leading Israel through the wilderness, and countless others who faced impossible situations yet trusted God.</p>
<p>When Abraham was called to leave everything he knew, he didn't have a detailed map or guarantee of success. He simply trusted God's promise. That's living by faith, not by sight.</p>
<h3>Practical Application</h3>
<p>In your daily life, this means making decisions based on God's Word rather than on your circumstances. It means praying when you're anxious, praising when you're struggling, and continuing forward even when the path is unclear. Living by faith doesn't mean ignoring reality; it means interpreting reality through the lens of God's promises.</p>
<p>Today, whatever you're facing—financial uncertainty, health concerns, relationship struggles, or career decisions—remember that God is faithful. He invites you to trust Him completely and step forward with confidence, knowing that He is with you.</p>
