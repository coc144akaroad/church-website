About Our Church

Grounded in faith, committed to community

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To glorify God, grow disciples of Jesus Christ, and serve our community with love, truth, and compassion. We believe in the transformative power of the Gospel and are committed to creating a welcoming space where all can encounter the grace of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To be a thriving, Spirit-led church that impacts our city for Christ through authentic worship, biblical teaching, and servant-hearted ministry. We envision a community where people experience transformation through faith and extend that love beyond our walls.

Our History

2005 - The Beginning

Church of Christ, Aka Road was founded with just 35 believers gathering in a small community center. Our founders dreamed of establishing a church that would center on Scripture and compassionate ministry.

2010 - Church Home Built

The congregation grew to 500+ members, and we purchased our current facility. This milestone allowed us to expand our ministries and better serve our community.

2015 - Global Outreach

We launched international mission partnerships and established a comprehensive discipleship program that transformed lives both locally and globally.

2020 - Digital Ministry Expansion

We embraced technology to reach and serve our congregation during challenging times, launching online services and expanded digital outreach.

2026 - Current Day

Over 2,000 members strong, Church of Christ, Aka Road continues its mission to glorify God, grow disciples, and serve our community with integrity and love.

What We Believe

The Authority of Scripture

We believe the Bible is God's authoritative, inerrant Word and is sufficient for all matters of faith and practice.

Salvation Through Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is the Son of God who died for our sins and rose again to provide eternal salvation to all who believe.

The Power of the Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit works in believers' lives to produce transformation, guidance, and fruit that glorifies God.

Discipleship & Growth

All believers are called to grow in their relationship with Christ and help others do the same.

The Great Commission

We are commanded to make disciples and share the Gospel with all nations, locally and globally.

Future Hope & Eternity

We believe Christ will return to establish His Kingdom, and all believers will spend eternity in His presence.
